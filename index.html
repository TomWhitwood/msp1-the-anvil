What is The Anvil?

The Anvil is more than a gym. It is a place where strength, confidence and resilience are shaped through hard work and determination. Whether you are taking your first strike or refining years of experience, The Anvil provides the tools, guidance, and community needed to help you forge a stronger body and a stronger self. Every workout at The Anvil is another step in crafting the person you aspire to become.

Who is The Anvil for?

Whether you are a beginner or an experienced gym goer, The Anvil is perfect for all ages, fitness levels and experience. We pride ourselves on maintaining a gym environment where everyone can feel welcome and comfortable. If you are completely new to a gym and feeling nervous, or perhaps don't know where to start, our qualified personal trainers, aka 'Smiths', are always available to answer any questions you have and to give you the help and support that you may need! Our Smiths can give you a guided tour of the facility, showing you all of our state of the art equipment and how to use it properly and safely, your safety and well-being is our top priority! We provide a wide range of classes that differ in terms of difficulty, perfectly tailored for all members and all fitness levels. 'Anvil Breaker' ice breaker sessions are included, but not mandatory, for all new members. This is a perfect opportunity to meet our amazing members of staff, other new gym members and potential future workout buddies.
